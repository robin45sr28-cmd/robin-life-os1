ROBIN LIFE OS V12.0 — TRANSLATE & EXPLAIN

AI Assistant now includes one-tap text actions:
- Translate to English
- Translate to Bangla
- Explain in Bangla
- Explain in English
- Fix grammar & explain

How to use:
1. Open AI Assistant.
2. Paste or type English/Bangla text into the question box.
3. Tap the desired action. The app sends the task and text to the AI automatically.
4. For full-quality answers, the hosted AI backend must be deployed, have OPENAI_API_KEY configured, and its URL saved in the app.

If no backend is configured, the app can only provide limited local fallback replies. No AI can guarantee every translation or explanation is perfect; review important text. Never put API keys in the public website files.
